# Privacy Policy for Starleet

_Last updated: June 29, 2025_

**Starleet** (operated from DTU, Copenhagen, Denmark) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data collected through the Starleet app.

## 1. Data We Collect

The Starleet app collects **anonymous performance and wellness data**, including:

- Heart rate
- Lactate levels
- Pace
- Power
- Distance
- Duration

We do **not** collect names, email addresses, user IDs, or any directly identifiable personal information.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use this anonymized data to provide you with wellness and performance insights. To do this, Starleet sends the data to a third-party artificial intelligence service (ChatGPT, operated by OpenAI) to generate responses and recommendations.

## 3. Data Sharing

Anonymous performance data is shared with ChatGPT (OpenAI) solely to support training feedback and recovery coaching. No personal identifiers are sent. Users are strongly advised **not** to include personal or sensitive information in chat messages.

## 4. Data Retention

Starleet does not permanently store this data on its own servers. The third-party processor (OpenAI) may temporarily retain data in accordance with its own privacy policy, which can be found at:  
[https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)

## 5. Consent and Control

By using the chat feature, you consent to sharing anonymized performance data with ChatGPT. If you do not wish to share data, you can refrain from using the chat feature or disable consent in the app’s settings.

## 6. Security

Data is transmitted securely using modern encryption standards (HTTPS). Starleet does not store personal identifiers or link the data to any user accounts.
